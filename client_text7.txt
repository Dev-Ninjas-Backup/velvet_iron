Hi team! During today's testing, I found a few additional issues that need to be addressed:

1. Exercise Scheduling: I scheduled Aerial Silks for October 15 at 7:15 PM for 50 minutes.
After saving, it displayed the wrong date, 12:15 AM, and 30 minutes. The edit screen also
showed incorrect information. Medication scheduling works correctly, so this appears
specific to exercise scheduling.

2. Step Quest Triggering Incorrectly: Logging Aerial Silks as a completed workout
automatically completed "Stride of the Realmwalker." This quest should only trigger when
the required steps are recorded in the step tracker, not when an unrelated workout is
logged.

3. Titan's Nourishment Quest: I logged a protein shake containing exactly 30g of protein,
but the quest requiring 30g+ protein did not complete. It should trigger automatically
when a qualifying food entry is logged.

4. Exercise History: When logging a completed workout, there is no option to select the
date or time it actually occurred. Users should be able to log previous workouts
accurately rather than having everything recorded on the current date.

5. Daily Quest Reset - HIGH PRIORITY: It appears the app may be using a different
timezone for daily resets. Daily quests should reset at midnight in the user's local
timezone. Please also verify that streaks, steps, water, nutrition, and medication tracking
use the correct local date.

Thank you!
